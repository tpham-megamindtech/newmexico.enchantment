Not every great New Mexico view requires a grueling hike. Just outside the town of White Rock, a short stroll leads to an overlook where the land falls away and the Rio Grande appears far below, threading through a canyon of dark volcanic rock. It's a big reward for very little effort.

## A canyon carved in basalt

The river here has cut a deep gorge through layers of black basalt, leaving sheer cliffs and a rugged canyon that stretches into the distance. The contrast of green river, dark rock, and blue sky is striking.

> A five-minute walk delivers a view most places would make you earn for hours.

## Easy access, big payoff

The main overlook is reached by a short, gentle path, making it ideal for families or anyone short on time. More ambitious hikers can descend into the canyon on steeper trails for a closer look.

## A basecamp for more

White Rock sits near Bandelier National Monument and the Los Alamos mesas, making it an excellent base for a few days of exploring the varied landscapes of the Pajarito Plateau.

**Field notes**

- The main overlook is an easy, family-friendly walk.
- Steeper trails descend into the canyon for the ambitious.
- Use it as a base for nearby Bandelier and Los Alamos.

Some of the best views in New Mexico ask almost nothing of you. The White Rock overlook is one of them — a grand canyon vista at the end of a short, easy path, and a perfect introduction to the drama of the Rio Grande.
