In the high desert, the overnight hours are when skin either repairs itself or loses more ground. With humidity near zero and the body dehydrating at altitude, New Mexico's estheticians say the evening routine matters even more than the morning one — and it's the step most people rush.

## Repair happens at night

Skin does much of its recovery while you sleep, and a well-chosen nighttime routine gives it the raw materials. In a climate this dry, that means locking in moisture before the arid night pulls it back out.

> In the desert, what you do at bedtime shows up on your face by morning.

## Layer and seal

The local approach is to layer a hydrating serum under a richer night cream, sealing everything with a final occlusive step if the skin is especially parched. A bedroom humidifier does the rest, quietly, all night long.

## Less is often more

Estheticians warn against overloading with active ingredients that can irritate already-stressed skin. A few well-chosen products, used consistently, beat a crowded shelf used haphazardly.

**Field notes**

- Apply products to slightly damp skin to trap moisture.
- Run a humidifier overnight, especially in winter.
- Don't overload on actives — consistency and simplicity win.

Get the evening right and you wake to skin that's calm and comfortable instead of tight and flaking. In New Mexico's demanding air, the night shift is where the real work gets done.
