Visit Albuquerque on almost any calm morning and you'll spot them: a few balloons drifting over the Rio Grande valley, far from the October crowds. The Fiesta may be the headline, but ballooning here is a year-round way of life, and the off-season flights have a charm all their own.

## An everyday wonder

For locals, a balloon or two floating over the river at dawn is simply part of the scenery. Commercial operators fly year-round, and the sight never quite loses its quiet magic, even for those who see it often.

> In Albuquerque, a balloon over the river at dawn is just another beautiful Tuesday.

## The river corridor

The Rio Grande's cottonwood bosque provides a lush green ribbon through the desert city, and floating above it — with the Sandias catching the sunrise — is a different, gentler experience than the packed Fiesta field.

## Any season, any morning

Cool, stable mornings make for the best flying, which means winter can be surprisingly ideal. Fewer crowds and crisp, clear air reward those who visit outside the October rush.

**Field notes**

- Commercial flights operate year-round, weather permitting.
- Winter mornings can offer the calmest, clearest flying.
- The Rio Grande bosque makes a lush backdrop below.

You don't need the Fiesta's spectacle to fall for Albuquerque's balloons. Come any quiet morning, look east toward the mountains, and there they'll be — drifting over the river as they always have.
