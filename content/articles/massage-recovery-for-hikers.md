New Mexico is a playground for the outdoorsy, and the price of all that adventure is paid in sore legs and stiff shoulders. A wave of sports-focused massage therapists has risen to meet the demand, specializing in getting hikers, climbers, and skiers back onto the trail.

## Not your relaxation massage

This is targeted, functional work — deeper pressure, focused on the calves, hips, and shoulders that take the brunt of a hard day outdoors. The goal is recovery and mobility, not just a pleasant hour.

> The summit is the reward; the massage the next day is what lets you climb again.

## Timed to your trip

Savvy travelers book a session for the day after a big hike or ski day, when the stiffness peaks. Therapists here are used to reading a body that's been pushed and knowing exactly where it's protesting.

## Keep moving

The best therapists send you off with a stretch or two and a reminder to hydrate. At altitude, recovery is as much about water and rest as it is about the table time.

**Field notes**

- Book recovery work for the day after your biggest effort.
- Speak up about problem areas — this work is targeted.
- Pair it with hydration and gentle stretching.

Adventure and recovery are two halves of the same trip. Fold a good sports massage into your New Mexico itinerary and your body will thank you on every mile that follows.
