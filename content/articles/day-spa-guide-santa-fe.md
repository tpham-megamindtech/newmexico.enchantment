A spa day sounds simple until you're standing in the lobby unsure of the etiquette. Santa Fe's high-desert spas reward those who plan a little, and knowing how to structure the hours turns a single treatment into a full day of genuine restoration.

## Arrive early, leave late

The treatment is only part of the value; the robe, the quiet lounge, the steam room, and the herbal tea are the rest. Arriving an hour early to ease in — and lingering afterward — is where the real relaxation lives.

> The mistake first-timers make is treating the spa like an appointment instead of a whole afternoon.

## Build the sequence

A sensible arc: warm up in the steam or sauna, take your massage or facial with muscles already loose, then cool down slowly in the relaxation room. Book treatments with breathing space between them, not back to back.

## Small courtesies

Silence your phone, keep your voice low, and tell your therapist your preferences before they begin, not after. Gratuity is customary; ask at the desk if you're unsure.

**Field notes**

- Book a window, not a slot — leave time on both ends.
- Warm the body before deep treatments for better results.
- Hydrate and skip the heavy lunch beforehand.

Done right, a spa day is less an errand than a small retreat. Plan the hours, surrender the schedule, and you'll leave feeling like you were away for a week.
