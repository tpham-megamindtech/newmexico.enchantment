Some of the finest food in Albuquerque doesn't have an address so much as a schedule. The city's food-truck scene has exploded, and the trucks parked outside breweries, office lots, and weekend markets are where a lot of the real cooking now happens.

## Chile on wheels

The classics travel well: breakfast burritos smothered to order, green-chile-loaded fries, carne adovada tacos handed through a window. Because trucks live and die on regulars, the chile is often as good as any sit-down spot.

> The best meal in Albuquerque might be waiting in a parking lot right now.

## A world of influences

Alongside the New Mexican staples, the trucks bring range — Oaxacan moles, Sonoran hot dogs, Vietnamese-Southwestern mashups. The low overhead lets cooks take risks a full restaurant can't.

## How to find them

Follow the trucks on social media, or graze a weekend market where several gather. Breweries frequently host a rotating truck, pairing local beer with whatever's on the griddle that night.

**Field notes**

- Follow favorites online — locations change daily.
- Brewery lots are reliable truck-hunting grounds.
- Bring cash; not every window takes cards.

It's fast, it's cheap, and it's often better than food twice the price. In Albuquerque, the humble truck has quietly become the city's most exciting place to eat.
