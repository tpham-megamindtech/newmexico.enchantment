Canyon Road may be the densest half-mile of art in America. Once a Pueblo footpath and later a farming lane, it now climbs from downtown Santa Fe past adobe compound after adobe compound, each one a gallery, until the whole street feels like a single rambling museum.

## Sculpture in every courtyard

Bronze horses, welded abstractions, and stone figures fill the gardens and gateways, meant to be touched and circled. Wandering the courtyards is free, unhurried, and open to everyone.

> Nowhere else can you meet a hundred artists' visions on a single afternoon stroll.

## From traditional to daring

The range is enormous — classic Western landscapes a few doors from sharp contemporary work. Wander first without an agenda, then double back to the pieces that stopped you.

## Friday-night art walks

On Friday evenings the galleries throw open their doors with openings and receptions, and the road fills with a convivial, glass-in-hand crowd. It's the liveliest way to experience the street.

**Field notes**

- Wear comfortable shoes — it's a gentle but steady uphill.
- Galleries are free to enter; there's no pressure to buy.
- The lower end has the best cafés for a mid-walk break.

Buy nothing or buy a masterpiece — either way, Canyon Road delivers the rare pleasure of art met on foot, in daylight, at your own pace.
