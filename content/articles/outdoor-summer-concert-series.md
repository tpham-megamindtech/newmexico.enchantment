There may be no better place to hear live music than a New Mexico amphitheater on a summer night. As the desert heat breaks and the stars emerge, the state's outdoor venues — from foothill amphitheaters to winery lawns — turn concerts into something close to magic.

## Venues with a view

The best outdoor stages are carved into dramatic settings, with mountain silhouettes and open skies as the backdrop. The scenery is often as memorable as the headliner, and the acoustics of a still desert night are hard to beat.

> A summer concert under a desert sky is one of New Mexico's great, underrated pleasures.

## The evening cool-down

Timing is everything: shows start as the fierce afternoon heat finally relents, and by the encore you may want a jacket. That daily swing from hot to cool is part of the high-desert concert experience.

## Make a night of it

Many venues pair music with local food and wine, and the relaxed, picnic-friendly vibe encourages you to arrive early. Bring layers, watch the sunset, and let the stars come out with the music.

**Field notes**

- Shows start as the heat breaks — bring a layer for later.
- Arrive early to enjoy the sunset and local food.
- The setting is half the show; choose your venue for the view.

Between the mountain backdrop, the cooling air, and the emerging stars, an outdoor show here engages far more than your ears. It's a full-body reminder of why summer nights in New Mexico are so cherished.
