Some of Santa Fe's best entertainment costs nothing at all. Through the summer, the Plaza bandstand hosts free live music several evenings a week, and locals and visitors alike gather on the grass and benches for everything from mariachi to jazz to flamenco under the cottonwoods.

## A stage for everyone

The bandstand programming is deliberately eclectic and open to all, reflecting the city's cultural mix. One night it's a Latin big band, the next a folk singer or a brass ensemble — and nobody checks for a ticket.

> The Plaza bandstand is proof that Santa Fe's best nights are often the free ones.

## The whole city shows up

The free format makes the Plaza wonderfully democratic on these evenings, mixing longtime residents, tourists, kids, and elders in one relaxed crowd. Bring a blanket, grab a snack nearby, and settle in.

## Golden-hour ambiance

As the music plays, the low sun warms the surrounding adobe and the temperature finally drops to comfortable. It's Santa Fe at its most easygoing and communal.

**Field notes**

- Performances run on summer evenings — check the local calendar.
- Bring a blanket; grassy and bench seating fills up fast.
- Grab dinner from a nearby spot and make an evening of it.

In a city that can feel pricey, the Plaza's summer music is a gift freely given. Show up, spread a blanket, and join the oldest kind of community gathering there is.
