Not every balloon at the Fiesta is a tidy teardrop. On Special Shape Rodeo days, the sky fills with the wonderfully absurd — enormous bees, floating castles, cartoon characters, and creatures of every kind — in what is easily the most crowd-pleasing session of the whole event.

## Engineering meets whimsy

Special shape balloons are marvels of design, holding their improbable forms through clever construction. Building and flying one is far harder than a standard envelope, and pilots take real pride in the crowd's delight.

> There is no cynicism left in anyone watching a giant balloon dinosaur lift into the sky.

## A hit with kids

If you're traveling with children, this is the session to prioritize. The parade of giant, friendly shapes lifting off one after another produces a running soundtrack of gasps and laughter across the field.

## Glow editions

On some evenings the special shapes gather for a glow, lit from within after dark. A three-story cartoon character aglow against the night sky is a sight that stays with you.

**Field notes**

- Special Shape Rodeo days are the most family-friendly sessions.
- Arrive early for a good field position with kids.
- Evening special-shape glows are worth staying for.

It's gloriously unserious, and that's the point. The Special Shape Rodeo is the Fiesta at its most purely joyful — proof that wonder doesn't always have to be solemn.
