Walk into a modern New Mexico skincare studio and you may find yourself under a mask of glowing lights. LED facial therapy — red, blue, and everything between — has arrived, wrapped in bold claims. We asked local estheticians to separate the genuine benefits from the marketing glow.

## What the colors claim

Red light is generally used to support collagen and calm inflammation; blue light targets the bacteria linked to breakouts. It's painless, requires no downtime, and feels like little more than lying under a warm, colorful lamp.

> LED therapy won't rewrite your face — but as one part of a routine, it quietly helps.

## Managing expectations

Practitioners are refreshingly honest: LED is a supporting player, not a miracle. Results come from consistent sessions over time, and it works best folded into a broader routine rather than treated as a standalone fix.

## Who benefits

Those dealing with occasional breakouts or seeking a gentle anti-aging boost tend to be happiest. If you want dramatic, overnight change, the honest local advice is to look elsewhere.

**Field notes**

- Consistency over weeks matters more than any single session.
- It pairs well with a hydrating facial, not as a replacement.
- Painless and downtime-free — an easy add-on to try.

The lights are soothing, the science is promising if modest, and the local verdict is measured: a useful tool in the kit, best judged over months rather than minutes.
